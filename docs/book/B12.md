# B12 · Applicative

**Denotes.** Independent effectful pieces combined — every error kept, not just the first. The step between functor and the dependent chain, and the answer for exactly the moment two validations know nothing about each other.

## 1 · The aha

A dependent chain short-circuits: the first failure hides the second, and the user fixes one field at a time. When the pieces are INDEPENDENT, the honest answer keeps both errors — the applicative combine is that answer, and the choice between it and the dependent chain is the design.
