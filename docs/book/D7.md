# D7 · The course judge: the Brief, the ledger, the triggers

**Denotes.** The goal is typed and confirmed once — the Brief — and the runtime's own counts decide when a judge looks; its verdict is one of three values, and a broken judge never blocks a turn.

## 1 · The aha

You confirm what was asked once, in four lines, before the work starts. The runtime does not watch every step: measured counts — the same call repeated past the guard, a stop after real work — decide when the judge wakes, at most once a turn.

The judge answers with a value: `aligned` (nothing happens), `minor_drift` (a note rides to the agent), `drift` (one question to you, with what it quotes).

## 2 · What it denotes

The **Brief** is the typed witness of a request: `goal · constraints · nonGoals · acceptance`. The draft is model-made, the card shows the four blocks, and your one tap (or one fixed line) settles it; a conversational turn earns no card. The confirmed text rides the turn as a system-reminder and is stored as the session's runtime-brief row.

A **trigger** is a measured predicate over the runtime's own event flow — never a timer, never a guess. Two exist today: the repeated-call streak twice past the static guard, and a stop after two or more model steps (judged at the next turn's start, so a dead attempt spawns no call from teardown).

The **verdict** is `aligned | minor_drift | drift`, each carrying what it quotes and, for minor drift, the one-fix note. A judge that fails, times out, or speaks without quoting is aligned: the turn proceeds and the failure is logged to be counted.

The snag: both gates are inert by construction outside the interactive app — the harness and the tests stay quiet, and the judge's only word to the agent is the persisted note.

## 4 · In your system

1. The Brief: the turn starts, the draft is model-made, the card asks confirm or fix — `ZCode/apps/zcode-cli/packages/core/src/runtime/methods/brief-gate.ts`.
2. The judge wakes: `ZCode/apps/zcode-cli/packages/core/src/runtime/methods/turn-tool-warnings.ts` calls it when the streak doubles the guard's count; `ZCode/apps/zcode-cli/packages/core/src/runtime/methods/interrupt-marker.ts` parks the stop's counts for the next turn's start.
3. The reading: `ZCode/apps/zcode-cli/packages/core/src/runtime/methods/verdict-gate.ts` makes the one call — goal, measured counts, a bounded window of recent steps — and dispatches on the tag.
4. The note rides: `ZCode/apps/zcode-cli/packages/core/src/system-reminder/source.ts` registers `verdict` as a persisted source that replays on resume, so an interrupt-time note speaks for the next attempt.
5. The count: every outcome is a structured log — `verdict.spoke`, `verdict.aligned`, `verdict.run.failed_open` — the raw rows `rumbo` will count later.
