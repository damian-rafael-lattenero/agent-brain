# N12 · `fmt.nu` — human numbers and widths

**Denotes.** The owner's number law as code: the raw → K → M ladder, the American system, the boundary that must not lie — plus the wrap that keeps wide tokens out of table cells.

## 1 · The aha

Quota and token numbers arrive RAW from the APIs: `300,000,000` means nothing on a phone screen. The owner corrected the ladder four times in one day — the law that survived: `.` for decimals, `,` for thousands, K and M rungs with trailing zeros trimmed, and the M rung fires AT the boundary — 1,000,000 is "1M", never "1000K". Nushell has no such formatter builtin (the gap verified on 0.117), so the law became the module.

## 2 · What it denotes

`human-num` (the ladder, tested with real magnitudes — the correction protocol's own receipt), `hard-wrap` (chunk any token longer than `max` into space-separated pieces — a wide table cell never holds an unbreakable token, the measured 405-char-border law). Verified: `999999` → `"1000K"`, `1000000` → `"1M"`, `166600` → `"166.6K"` — the boundary fires exactly where the law says.
