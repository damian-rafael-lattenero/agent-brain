# B25 · Denotational design

**Denotes.** Choosing first what each type MEANS — mathematically, in one sentence — and only then the equations and the code that compute that meaning.

## 1 · The aha

"Software design is the posing of tasteful algebra problems; software implementation is the correct solution of those problems" (Conal Elliott). Two instances can share a signature and mean different things; what rejects the evil one is not the type checker but a meaning — the functor laws say `fmap` changes only the elements, never the structure.

## 2 · What it denotes

The method (Elliott, LambdaJam 2015): 1 form first; 2 choose the denotation — precise and adequate, stripped of implementation bias; 3 specify by equations; 4 the homomorphism principle — the meaning of the whole follows from the meanings of the parts, and a FAILING LAW IS A DESIGN SIGNAL: redesign the denotation, never patch the code; 5 derive the implementation.

## 3 · In your languages

**Kotlin.** The fleet's citable example: kap's `Validated` — its denotation (accumulate ALL errors) forces applicative-NOT-monad, because a bind would short-circuit.
