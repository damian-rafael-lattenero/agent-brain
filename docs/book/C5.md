# C5 · Embeddings from scratch: vector, cosine, 768, HNSW

**Denotes.** A text becomes 768 numbers whose angles carry meaning: two passages about the same thing point the same way, and nearness is measured as the cosine of the angle — never the distance of the raw numbers.

## 1 · The aha

Meaning as direction. "A monad is a monoid" and "monads are monoids in disguise" land near each other in angle even though they share few words — which is exactly what word matching misses and the vector leg catches.

## 2 · What it denotes

The model (nomic-embed-text, the fleet's choice) maps any text to a fixed-width vector — `embed_dim` = 768 in the brain's config. Similarity is cosine: dot product over the product of lengths, so only direction matters, not magnitude.

Near-neighbor search at corpus scale is its own problem: exact scan compares the question against every stored vector; an ANN index such as HNSW trades exactness for speed with a graph of near neighbors. The fleet runs the exact scan today — C6 records why: the query parameters hide the table's size from the HNSW planner, and exact beats approximate until the corpus outgrows it.
