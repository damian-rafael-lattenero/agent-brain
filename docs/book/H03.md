# H03 · Natural transformation: a uniform translation

**Denotes.** A family of arrows between two functors, one component per object, that turns every arrow of the source into a commuting square — uniformity as a condition, not a hope.

## 1 · The aha

Any `F a → G a` per type is easy; the condition is that the translation commutes with every arrow. In Haskell the condition is paid for by a restriction: one parametric formula for all types, "we are not touching the items themselves" — and parametricity makes such a formula natural automatically.

## 2 · What it denotes

Two functors `F, G : C → D`; components `α_a : F a → G a`, one per object; and for every `f : a → b`: `G f ∘ α_a = α_b ∘ F f` — one commuting square per arrow. In Haskell there is no class: `alpha :: forall a. F a -> G a`, and `fmap f . alpha = alpha . fmap f` (two different fmaps).

"Any polymorphic function of the type ... automatically satisfies the naturality condition" — the free theorem of parametricity, the same machinery B19 drilled. The catch: the theorem needs one formula for ALL types, while a class-constrained function is given per instance, and the mirrored manuals state nothing for that case. Full theory: `theory-kb/notes/category-theory/natural-transformation.md`.

## 3 · In your languages

**Kotlin.** Arrow's typed-errors story is a conversion family in the wild: functions with a `Raise<E>` receiver "can be transformed into a variety of types; not only `Either` and `Ior`, but also `Result`, `Option`, or a nullable type" — the pages read state no uniformity law, so the square is a design promise, not a compiler one. Full row: `kotlin-kb/notes/canon-map.md`, `parametricity`.

**Nushell.** Reshaping is one command per concrete shape — `flatten` "flatten a table by extracting nested values", `zip` "combine a stream with the input", `collect` "collect a stream into a value" — and the type-signature chapter lists no generic conversion family: the honest ceiling, uniformity by convention instead of by construction. Full row: `nushell-kb/notes/canon-map.md`, `parametricity`.
