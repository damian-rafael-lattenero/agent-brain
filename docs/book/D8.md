# D8 · Correction, proposal, promotion

**Denotes.** Every way you correct an agent becomes a bus event, the machine turns repeated corrections into proposed rules, and promotion — a proposal becoming a rule — happens only when you decide it.

## 1 · The aha

The correction loop has three stations and one human gate. First the correction itself is a typed event on the bus — not a mood, not a scolding: a row. Second, the machine may cluster and propose; a judge cause can name a missing rule. Third, promotion is yours alone: nothing writes a KB, a hook, or the corpus by itself.

## 2 · What it denotes

A **correction** is `kind='correction'` on the wire, payload `{"kind": "steer" | "stop" | "cancel" | "explicit", "text": "..."}` — your steering words, a stop, a cancel; the attribution ladder is live turn, else last sealed turn, else unknown. Ingestion is idempotent (the row id IS the event id), and the table is `sid · turn · kind · text · candidate · status · timestamps`.

A **proposal** is what the machine earns: `CorrectionExtractor` reads your words once — same resilience family as memory, one chat call — and writes one rule sentence into `candidate`, flipping the row to `proposed`. A blank candidate records the attempt and stays pending: an honest queue entry, never a pretended proposal. The corpus doctor's `law_gap` cause names the same shape from the other side — a retrieval failure that is really a missing rule, proposed as a rule sentence.

**Promotion** is `agent-brain corrections accept <id>` — yours, by hand, from `proposed` to `accepted`. The accepted rule sentence lives in the table; making it a hook or a KB note is a separate, explicitly approved step. Nothing in the loop writes those on its own.

## 3 · In your languages

Kotlin: the tap in `zcode-bot-kotlin/modules/daemon/src/main/kotlin/dev/lattenero/bot/daemon/bus/BusProducer.kt` mirrors `feedback`; the wiring rides `ChannelWiring.kt`'s ops binding (steer carries your words, stop and cancel carry none); the brain side ingests in `PgBusIngest.kt`, extracts in `CorrectionExtractor.kt`, drains in `CorrectionWorker.kt`, and the store is `Corrections.sq`.

Nushell: the decision surface — the CLI verb is thin by design: list, accept, reject, each printing what it did; the counts live in Postgres one fold away.

The difference: Kotlin observes and proposes, you promote. The snag found live: feedback's tap swallowed its own failure for weeks and the 👍/👎 recorded zero rows — the counts are the witness, so every tap's silence must be checkable by one query.

## 4 · In your system

1. The taps: steer, stop, cancel — `zcode-bot-kotlin/modules/daemon/src/main/kotlin/dev/lattenero/bot/daemon/ChannelWiring.kt`, the `onCorrection` binding beside `onFeedback`.
2. The contract: `agent-brain/core/src/main/kotlin/dev/lattenero/brain/bus/BusEvent.kt` (`CORRECTION` in the taxonomy) and `agent-brain/core/src/main/kotlin/dev/lattenero/brain/bus/CorrectionEvent.kt` (the payload's sum).
3. The mirror: `agent-brain/core/src/main/sqldelight/dev/lattenero/brain/db/sq/Corrections.sq` — insert-or-ignore by id, `markExtracted` flips pending to proposed only on a non-blank candidate.
4. The worker: `agent-brain/core/src/main/kotlin/dev/lattenero/brain/memory/CorrectionWorker.kt` — bounded batches, settled one by one, degraded rows retried next pass.
5. The doctor: `agent-brain/core/src/main/kotlin/dev/lattenero/brain/eval/CorpusDoctor.kt` — `LAW_GAP` in the cause vocabulary, the proposal is a rule sentence.
6. The gate: `agent-brain/core/src/main/kotlin/dev/lattenero/brain/Main.kt` — `corrections list | accept <id> | reject <id>`, guarded decisions only.
