# B13 · Monad

**Denotes.** The dependent chain — each step's shape decided by the previous step's value — with three equations (left identity, right identity, associativity) that make the chain as refactorable as any pipeline.

## 1 · The aha

The first value decides what the next computation even IS — that dependency is why one operator (`andThen`, `>>=`, `bind`) can chain a lookup that may miss into a render that needs it, and why the laws matter: they let you pull the chain apart and re-lay it without changing the answer.

## 2 · What it denotes

In Haskell: `class Applicative m => Monad m where (>>=) :: m a -> (a -> m b) -> m b` — laws pinned by the executable law run `Monad.hs`: left identity `return a >>= f == f a`, right identity `m >>= return == m`, associativity. do-notation is sugar over exactly this chain.
