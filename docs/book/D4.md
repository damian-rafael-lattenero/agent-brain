# D4 · The ladder: fact, skill, hook

**Denotes.** A lesson climbs: it lands where the fact lives (the brain), rises to a procedure (a skill), hardens into a mechanical law (a deny script), and the law file keeps the rule itself — each rung with its proof.

## 1 · The aha

The same lesson belongs at different rungs at different times, and the mistake is skipping: a fact kept as chat history dies with the session; a procedure kept as prose is re-derived wrong; a mechanical law kept only in the law file is trusted, not enforced. The ladder says where each thing lives NOW.

## 2 · What it denotes

The rungs, bottom to top: the brain keeps the FACT (memory keeps what happened); a skill keeps the PROCEDURE (how to repeat it); a hook keeps the MECHANICAL LAW (the harness obeys it); AGENTS.md keeps the LAW (the rule, one home, cited and measured by `fleet agents-check`).

Promotion is the ladder's verb: the owner's second time fixing the same thing becomes a candidate rule — proposed with the proof that earned it, never applied without the owner's word, and a rule leaves when a deny script enforces it or its reason is gone. The event-bound waits law rode every rung in one unit: a brain memory, then the KB note, then the warn path in the hook, then the law line.

## 3 · In your languages

**Kotlin.** The ladder's Kotlin twin: detekt rules for the mechanical laws, tests for the procedures, the KB notes for the facts. Full row: `kotlin-kb/notes/canon-map.md`, `type-classes`.

**Nushell.** The law tests ride with the code: `nu ~/Scripts/test.nu --ci` runs every script's test — a mechanical law without its test is a finding. Full row: `nushell-kb/notes/canon-map.md`, `type-classes`.
