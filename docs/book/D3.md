# D3 · Skill: the procedure

**Denotes.** A skill is a procedure the harness loads on demand: a SKILL.md with frontmatter that says what it covers and when to load it — the how-to layer between the law file and the code.

## 1 · The aha

The law says WHAT must hold; the skill says HOW, step by step, when the work reaches that shape. Loading is the design win: a skill costs nothing until its loading rule matches, then the whole procedure rides the context.

## 2 · What it denotes

Shape: a directory with SKILL.md, frontmatter (name, description — the loading rule), the body as steps. The fleet carries two: the design law's long form and the health battery's runbook. Skills are created with `skill-creator`, under `~/.agents/skills/` for every project or `<repo>/.agents/skills/` for that repo only.

The boundary against the other layers: the law file (AGENTS.md) states the rule; a KB note holds the verified fact; the skill carries the procedure — the ladder's middle rung (D4 names the whole ladder).

## 3 · In your languages

**Kotlin.** The runbook (`zcode-bot-kotlin/docs/runbook.md`) plays the skill's role for the bot: procedures with proofs, versioned with the code. Full row: `kotlin-kb/notes/canon-map.md`, `maybe-either`.

**Nushell.** The same idea lives in comment headers: a script's opening lines carry its usage and laws — `Scripts/scripts/hooks/oracle-compile.nu`'s LAWS section is a skill's body in miniature. Full row: `nushell-kb/notes/canon-map.md`, `maybe-either`.
