# H06 · Monoidal functor: effects side by side

**Denotes.** A functor that combines side by side: a tensor in the source becomes a comparison map in the target, the unit becomes a unit-shaped value — and none of it promises a way back (lax, one way).

## 1 · The aha

B12 gave you applicative as `pure` + `<*>`. The categorical formulation is the same pair with its meaning showing: `unit :: f ()` and `(**) :: f a -> f b -> f (a, b)` — both operands are values from the start, which is exactly why two independent validations run side by side while a monadic bind hides the second.

## 2 · What it denotes

A functor between monoidal categories with a tensor map `F a ⊗ F b → F (a, b)` and a unit map `i → F i`, compatible with the associator and unitors — in Haskell, `class Functor f => Monoidal f where unit :: f (); (**) :: f a -> f b -> f (a, b)`, and "`pure` and `(<*>)` are equivalent in power to `unit` and `(**)`".

Lax means one way: "there aren't necessarily functions in the other direction, like `f (a, b) -> (f a, f b)`". Two honest approximations: the laws hold "up to isomorphism rather than equality" (`(x, ())` ≅ `x`), and the naturality law "in the context of Haskell ... is a free theorem", not a check anyone runs.

The interchange law is the side-by-side reading: with `(<*>)` "the order in which we evaluate the function and its argument doesn't matter"; a bind `(>>=) :: m a -> (a -> m b) -> m b` builds its second from the first's result, so the first failure hides the second. Full theory: `theory-kb/notes/category-theory/monoidal-functor.md`.

## 3 · In your languages

**Kotlin.** Arrow names both routes: fail-fast, and accumulation for "computations whose failure is independent of each other" — "if you want to accumulate errors instead, you should use `zipOrAccumulate`, or `mapOrAccumulate`". Full row: `kotlin-kb/notes/canon-map.md`, `applicative`.

**Nushell.** `zip` "combine a stream with the input" is the tensor-shaped step; `par-each` runs rows in parallel with `--keep-order`: "keep sequence of output same as the order of input" — side by side, order kept. No law is documented; the promise lives in the laws B12 drilled. Full row: `nushell-kb/notes/canon-map.md`, `applicative`.
