# D10 · The seal: the shape of the answer

**Denotes.** The delivered work is measured once against the confirmed Brief and one answer shape — `result · evidence · next` — and a miss sends the turn back once; the second miss is yours.

## 1 · The aha

"Done" is not a feeling; it is a measurement. When the turn that carries a Brief finishes, the seal reads the delivered reply and asks two things only: is every acceptance statement checkable as done, and does the reply carry its three parts — the result, the proof, the next step.

One miss costs one bounce: the turn goes back with the gaps written down. The second miss is not the seal's decision anymore — it is a notice on your card, and only your tap sends the work back again.

## 2 · What it denotes

`seal : Brief + Reply -> Check`, with `Check = { complete, replyShapeOk, gaps }`. Uncertainty fails: an acceptance line the reply cannot prove is not met. A seal that fails, times out, or speaks nonsense passes the turn — it measures, it never traps, and the failure is logged to be counted.

The shape is the law of the answer from the system prompt: what was done, what proves it (a command, a test, a number, a cited file), and the one next step. The seal does not grade prose style; it refuses an answer with no proof or no direction.

## 3 · In your languages

TypeScript in the fork: the pure measure in `ZCode/apps/zcode-cli/packages/contracts/src/tools/seal.ts` — the prompt with Brief and reply as escaped data, the fail-open parse (only an explicit `false` fails; silence passes), the bounce note, the notice card and its three answers.

The gate in `ZCode/apps/zcode-cli/packages/core/src/runtime/methods/seal-gate.ts` runs where a stop hook would, in `turn-stop.ts`: only on a turn with a confirmed Brief, never stacked on a stop hook's own continuation, one measurement per finish, one bounce per turn.

The snag: the bounce must carry the gaps, not a grade — "the test was never run" sends the agent back to work; "quality is low" sends it nowhere.

## 4 · In your system

1. The measure fires at the natural finish of a Brief turn — `ZCode/apps/zcode-cli/packages/core/src/runtime/methods/turn-stop.ts`, after the stop-hook lane.
2. The bounce note rides as the `seal` reminder source, turn-scoped — `ZCode/apps/zcode-cli/packages/core/src/system-reminder/source.ts`.
3. The second miss opens the card: end the turn, or one more bounce with your own words.
4. Every outcome is a structured log — `seal.passed`, `seal.bounced`, `seal.owner_return`, `seal.owner_end` — plus the session's runtime-seal row; the raw rows the numbers phase will count.
