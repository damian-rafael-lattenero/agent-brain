# H04 · Limit and colimit: products and sums freed from syntax

**Denotes.** The universal cone over a diagram — the object carrying exactly the diagram's information, through which every other cone factors uniquely; a colimit is the same with every arrow reversed.

## 1 · The aha

B06 gave you pairs and tagged unions as syntax. The universal property is WHY they are what they are: a product is not "a type with two projections" but the one all others factor through uniquely — uniqueness is what bans the junk.

## 2 · What it denotes

A diagram `D : I → C` and a cone over it — a natural transformation from a constant functor to `D`, one leg per object — with the limit as "the universal cone as the terminal object in the category of cones": every other cone has a unique `m : c' → Lim[D]` factorizing its legs. Colimit: invert every arrow. The product lands as `(a, b)` with `factorizer p q = \x -> (p x, q x)`; the coproduct as "a tagged union of two types" — `Either e a`.

Two honest approximations: a limit "doesn't always exist, but when it does, it is unique up to a unique isomorphism", so `(a, b)` and a two-field record are both representatives; and Haskell has no limit of a general diagram — the mirrored manuals do not cover colimits, pullbacks or pushouts. Full theory: `theory-kb/notes/category-theory/limit-colimit.md`.

## 3 · In your languages

**Kotlin.** The same two shapes under different names: `Pair<A, B>` and a data class's fields are the product; Arrow's `Either<E, A>` "only admits these two possibilities: a `Left` holding a value of type `E` or a `Right` holding a value of type `A`" is the coproduct, with `null` as a sum of one. Full row: `kotlin-kb/notes/canon-map.md`, `sum-product-types`.

**Nushell.** Both directions live in the type-signature chapter: compound types `record<type: int, bar: string>` and `table<name: string, size: filesize>` are the product shape; union types — "`oneof<int, string>` matches a value of any of the listed types" — are the coproduct shape, written in the signature itself. Full row: `nushell-kb/notes/canon-map.md`, `sum-product-types`.
